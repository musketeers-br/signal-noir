Feature: Approval and the transactional outbox.

Describe the behavior:
1. An Approval binds an immutable Proposal: Action, parameters, transformed payload and the versions it relied on. It records author, moment, proposal version and decision.
2. Outside a BenchmarkRun, every Action with an external effect requires a valid recorded Approval. HOLD does not. Automatic Approval exists only inside a BenchmarkRun, is emitted by the process that opens the Case, carries actor `benchmark`, and is labelled. The Benchmark runner never approves.
3. Approval has a deadline. An expired or materially incompatible Approval must not execute. New evidence alone does not expire an Approval unless it falsifies a declared precondition.
4. Concurrent Approvals of the same version result in exactly one effective authorization. A repeated Approval returns the existing Approval idempotently and reports `idempotentReplay`. A stale or superseded Proposal is refused. A conflict returns 409 and records nothing.
5. Denial moves the Case to Rejected. The first denial may start a new Investigation with the reason as evidence; a second denial ends in ReviewRequired.
6. Every transition that requires downstream work writes, in the same short transaction, the new state, the timeline event and an outbox entry containing commandId, type, caseId and caseVersionAfter. I/O happens after commit, never inside the transaction.
7. After commit, the transition owner dispatches the outbox entry to a single command entry point. A sweeper redelivers pending entries. The processor handles each commandId at most once and checks caseVersionAfter against the Case.
8. Every transition has an authorized actor. An actor not listed for that transition receives INVALID_ACTOR. The actor `benchmark` is refused outside a BenchmarkRun.
9. If the authorization cannot be recorded, the Execution must not happen.

Acceptance scenarios:
- Approve the exact version: Approved, approval persisted, survives restart.
- Approve a stale version: 409, nothing recorded.
- Approve twice: one effective authorization, idempotent replay reported.
- Two operators approve concurrently: exactly one effective authorization.
- Approval expires: no execution, and a new Proposal/Approval is required.
- New evidence that does not affect a precondition: the Approval remains valid.
- First denial: new Investigation. Second denial: ReviewRequired.
- State, timeline event and outbox entry commit atomically; a lost dispatch is redelivered; a duplicate commandId produces no second effect.
- Unauthorized actor: INVALID_ACTOR.
- Persistence failure during authorization: no downstream effect.

Out of scope: Revalidation and Execution (008), the operator console, Validator rules.

Success criteria: no external effect is authorized without a durable, exact-version Approval; concurrent or repeated approvals never produce two authorizations; no committed transition loses its downstream work.

Anchors: contracts/openapi-signalnoir.yaml (decision, approvals, error codes).
